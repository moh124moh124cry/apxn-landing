One APXN Ecosystem

APXN Points are not a separate coin.

APXN Points are ecosystem activity points connected to your participation in the APXN project. Your balance appears through the APXN website Dashboard and the Telegram Mini App. APXN Points will be an important factor in future eligibility and APXN allocation when the official distribution phase begins.

Important

APXN Points are not currently an on-chain token balance. Final allocation, distribution and conversion rules will be announced officially before withdrawals are activated. No fixed conversion rate is active today.
