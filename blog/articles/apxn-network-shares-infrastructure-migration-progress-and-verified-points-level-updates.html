🎁 MEMBERSHIP & LOYALTY KYC | Q1 2027

✅ Every eligible user who successfully completes all required KYC tasks will instantly receive 20,000 Verified APXN Points, credited directly to their balance.

🚀 This allows new users to reach Level 1 immediately!

⛏️ After the first 10,000 participants have received their allocation, the 20,000 Verified APXN Points reward will remain available to all eligible users, with no limit on the number of recipients.

💰 63,000,000 APXN FOR THE COMMUNITY

63,000,000 APXN are allocated to the community, distributed through the 7-level system proportionally to Verified APXN Points within each level.
