Blockchain Data and Network Design

Ethereum relies on a proof of stake consensus mechanism where participants stake ETH to add new blocks. Validators run software and receive random selection to propose blocks that others verify before addition to the chain. Rewards and penalties encourage honest behavior and consistent availability. The overall design keeps the network secure through these economic incentives tied directly to the native currency. The mechanism selects participants based on the amount of collateral committed. This selection process distributes responsibility for maintaining the record across the active set.
