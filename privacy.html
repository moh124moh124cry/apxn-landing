Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Apex Network operates an official website and a Telegram Mini App. Depending on the feature used, project systems process Telegram account identifiers and profile fields, APXN Points activity, referral and task history, and account-related records such as linked wallet addresses or eligible reward transactions. Please do not send private keys, wallet recovery phrases or passwords to APXN support.

2. Use of Information

Account information is used to operate the APXN Mini App, maintain participation balances, process available tasks and referrals, support account and wallet functions, investigate abuse and provide support. Future Membership & Loyalty KYC stages will have their own officially published participation conditions. Participation in KYC is not a claim that government identity-document checks are currently active.

3. Data Security & RLS

APXN uses access controls and backend security measures, including Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) where configured. No internet service can guarantee absolute security; contact the project if you believe your account or data has been exposed.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@apxn.network
