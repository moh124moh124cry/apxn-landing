## Seven Levels & Membership and Loyalty KYC

The community allocation is divided across **seven levels (L1–L7)**, each with a dedicated APXN pool. Within a level, eligible users receive a **proportional share based on verified APXN Points relative to total verified points within that same level**, rather than equal amounts per user. The level and individual share will be shown on the Dashboard only after completion of **every stage of APXN Membership & Loyalty KYC**. Final allocations per level will be announced before the planned distribution and listing.

Official Early Access Badge rules, including eligibility, activity, benefits and retention conditions, will be published separately.
