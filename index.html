Community Distribution

Seven Levels & 63 Million APXN

The community allocation of 63,000,000 APXN is divided across seven levels, L1–L7. Each level has a dedicated token pool. A user’s level is determined by verified APXN Points.

Within a level, eligible users share that level’s allocation proportionally to their own verified APXN Points compared with the combined verified points of all eligible users in that level. It is not an equal-per-person split.

L1L2L3L4L5L6L7

Individual levels and APXN allocation will appear in the Dashboard only after completion of every stage of APXN Membership & Loyalty KYC. Final allocations for the seven levels will be officially announced before the distribution and listing described in the roadmap.

Planned Community Programs

Membership & Loyalty KYC · Badge · Airdrops

Q1 2027: Planned launch of APXN Membership & Loyalty KYC. After completing the required Membership & Loyalty KYC tasks and the displayed Telegram Stars payment, eligible accounts receive a one-time 20,000 verified APXN Points reward without a recipient cap. The first 10,000 qualifying Badge holders also receive a limited planned allocation of 100 APXN; once those slots are exhausted, the verified-points completion reward continues.

The first-airdrop amount displayed in an account is not an immediate blockchain transfer. The first-airdrop claim is targeted for Q2 2027 under the official claim rules.

Q2 2027: The planned second airdrop draws approximately 2,000,000 APXN from the 15 million APXN partnerships, marketing, development and airdrop fund. Claims for both airdrops are planned to open that quarter, subject to announced requirements.

APXN will separately publish official Early Access Badge instructions addressing eligibility, continued activity, benefits and retention conditions. Final rules are not yet published.

Launchpad, DEX & Mainnet

Q3 2027 targets APXN DEX and a CEX Launchpad. Q4 2027 targets an APXN Testnet and planned CEX/DEX listings. Q1 2028 covers mainnet preparation and new products; the APXN Mainnet launch is targeted for Q2 2028.

Launchpad pricing, participation conditions and eligible exchanges will be announced only when confirmed. No live public token sale, guaranteed listing, token distribution or withdrawal is implied.
